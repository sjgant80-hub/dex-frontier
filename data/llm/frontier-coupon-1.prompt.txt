You are predicting whether a driver accepts a coupon offered to them on the way somewhere. The data are real answers to a driving survey (the UCI In-Vehicle Coupon Recommendation dataset): each answer is one driver in one situation, offered one coupon. Each answer is described by:
- destination: where they are driving
- passanger: who is in the car
- weather: weather
- temperature: temperature (Fahrenheit)
- time: time of day
- coupon: the coupon offered
- expiration: how long the coupon lasts (2h or 1d)
- gender: gender
- age: age band
- maritalStatus: marital status
- has_children: 1 = has children
- education: education
- occupation: occupation
- income: yearly income band
- Bar: bar visits a month (never, less1, 1~3, 4~8, gt8; blank = not answered)
- CoffeeHouse: coffee house visits a month (same scale)
- CarryAway: take-away food a month (same scale)
- RestaurantLessThan20: restaurant meals under $20 a month (same scale)
- Restaurant20To50: restaurant meals of $20–50 a month (same scale)
- toCoupon_GEQ15min: 1 = the coupon’s place is at least 15 minutes’ drive
- toCoupon_GEQ25min: 1 = at least 25 minutes’ drive
- direction_same: 1 = the place is in the same direction they are driving
