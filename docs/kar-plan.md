### Step 1: One Specialist Body
**Objective:** Grow a single, verified-pattern SLM for a specific domain.
- **Resources Needed:** A device (like a smartphone or laptop) with sufficient CPU power.
- **Process:**
  1. **Seed Initialization:** Create a seed with the necessary genome (code-atoms, gate, growth-rules, Ψ-stub, small imagination-model reference).
  2. **Germination:** Assemble the SLM on the device using the seed's genome.
  3. **Verification:** Perform real-world tasks to grow the SLM's library (RSI) and ensure it outperforms a generalist model on its domain.
- **Outcome:** A functional, verified SLM that can perform domain-specific tasks.

### Step 2: The Composition-Proof
**Objective:** Prove that a mesh of specialists can compose into more than the sum.
- **Resources Needed:** 3-5 specialist seed-bodies, a network to route and compose their outputs.
- **Process:**

  2. **Coupling and Routing:** Couple the seeds at κ and route tasks to the appropriate specialists.
  3. **Composition Testing:** Measure the composed output against single specialists and a frontier baseline on cross-domain tasks.
- **Outcome:** Demonstrate that the composed output beats any single specialist and approaches a frontier model's capabilities.
